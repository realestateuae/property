Can international buyers purchase on Palm Jumeirah? +
Absolutely. Freehold ownership is open to all nationalities on Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Business Bay, JBR and more. Our legal team ensures seamless transfer.
What is the expected ROI for Business Bay? +
Business Bay yields 7–9% gross rental returns, with high demand for short-term lets due to central location & proximity to Downtown.

Investing AED 2M+ in ready property grants 10-year renewable Golden Visa, including spouse & children. Fast track process with our partners.
